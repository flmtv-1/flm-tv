Viewer Help

How to watch, find the TV Guide, and what to do if something isn't working.

We're still building

FLM TV is a new, independent network based in Las Vegas, and our website and apps are still under construction. While we add new features, you may now and then see a show freeze, a page load slowly, or something look different than it did yesterday. We keep a close eye on the channel and fix problems quickly. Thank you for your patience while we grow!

If a show freezes or stops

Most interruptions clear up in a minute or two. Try these in order:

On a computer

Refresh the page: press F5 (or Ctrl+R; on a Mac ⌘+R), then press Play again. If the page looks odd after an update, do a full refresh with Ctrl+Shift+R (Mac: ⌘+Shift+R).

On a phone or tablet

Tap your browser's reload button, or pull down from the top of the page to refresh. Still stuck? Close the browser tab and open flmtv.com again.

On Fire TV

Press Back on your remote, then choose Watch Live again. If it's still frozen, close the FLM TV app and reopen it.

On Roku

Press Back, then select the live channel again. If that doesn't work, press Home and reopen FLM TV.

Still not working?

Wait a couple of minutes and try again. If we're fixing something, it's usually back shortly. It also helps to check your own internet by opening another video or website.

Watching Channel 26.5 with an antenna

Over-the-air reception depends on things outside the network itself: your antenna, the weather, and the broadcast station's equipment.

Adjust your antenna

Move it higher or closer to a window, and turn it slightly. Small changes can make a big difference.

Rescan your channels

On your TV, open Settings and look for Channel Scan or Auto Program (the name varies by TV brand). Run the scan, then tune back to 26.5.

Bad weather

Wind, storms and heavy rain can break up the picture. It normally returns on its own once the weather clears.

Station maintenance

Sometimes the broadcast station has a technical issue or does maintenance. The channel comes back as soon as it's fixed.

Tip: If your antenna signal is down, you can watch the same channel live at flmtv.com, on Roku, or in the Fire TV app.

How to use FLM TV

See what's on now and what's coming up. Look for the gold TV GUIDE button.

Watch Live

Press Watch Live on the home page to watch Channel 26.5 as it airs.

Movies & shows

Scroll the rows on the home page, or open Browse Categories. Tap View All to see everything in a category.

Search

Type a title in the search bar near the top of the home page (computer and phone).

My List

Sign up free, then tap the on any movie or show to save it to your own list.

Chat

Talk with other viewers live. Enter your name first, then type your message.

Kids Corner

Family-friendly shows and movies, all in one place.

Parental Controls

Tap the lock icon to set a 4-digit parent code.

TV Stations
