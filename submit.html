Your Vision, Our Airwaves

Stop waiting in the wings. FLM TV is looking for independent filmmakers, series creators and music video artists ready to step into the spotlight. There's no fee to submit. "Grow with us"

What We're Looking For

Short Films

Any genre, under 40 minutes.

Feature Films

Indie features ready for broadcast.

Series & Pilots

Web series, talk, lifestyle, comedy, docuseries.

Music Videos

Independent artists with fully cleared music.

How It Works

  • Submit for free. Send a private screener link using the form below.
  • We review. Our team watches every submission and replies to selected creators.
  • Simple agreement. Selected creators sign a non-exclusive license. You keep ownership and can still distribute anywhere else.
  • You air. Your work airs on Channel 26.5 in Las Vegas and streams worldwide on flmtv.com, with on-air credit and promotion.

Before You Submit

  • You own the work or have the rights to license it.
  • All music is original, licensed, or royalty-free, with paperwork to prove it.
  • Everyone on camera has signed a release.
  • You can provide an HD file (1080p preferred) and, ideally, a caption file (SRT or SCC).

Submit Your Work
